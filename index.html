Factify — Gamified Social Media Strategy

Combating the viral spread of misinformation across Pakistan through interactive Instagram gamification, youth-centered design, and UN SDG alignment.

@thefactify.pk Youth (Ages 15–30) Islamabad • Lahore • Karachi • Rawalpindi

Transforming Media Literacy Into an Addictive, Gamified Social Experience

The Epidemic of Digital Misinformation

Pakistani youth on social media face a barrage of manipulated narratives, misleading headlines, and WhatsApp forwards. Traditional educational campaigns are passive, dry, and widely ignored by digital natives.

  • Core Problem: Confirmation bias and lack of instant critical verification before sharing.
  • Target Audience: High-density urban student and early-career demographic across Pakistan.
  • Key Objective: Inoculate audiences cognitively through interactive game mechanics.

Inoculation Theory & Gamified Quizzing

Instead of lecturing, Factify stepped players into the role of a digital investigative journalist. Using Instagram Reels, Stories, and interactive polls, real viral headlines were presented with instant "Real or Fake" mechanics.

  • Instant Feedback Loop: Explanations provided immediately after voting to build analytical muscle.
  • Bilingual Accessibility: Content crafted in both Urdu & English for nationwide resonance.
  • Visual Identity: Trust-evoking Blue & Golden Yellow accents for psychological engagement.
